Expense Flow

Privacy Policy

Expense tracking should feel personal. This policy explains what Expense Flow handles on your device and what is sent to online services when you use specific features.

Effective date: 11 October 2026  ·  Last updated: 11 October 2026

At a glance: Expense Flow stores your expense records on your device and processes supported bank/payment SMS messages locally on your device. The app connects to the internet only for two features: fetching live rates and submitting feedback. Your transaction records and SMS contents are not sent to us or to these services.

1. Who this policy applies to

This Privacy Policy applies to the Expense Flow Android application (“Expense Flow,” “the app,” “we,” or “our”).

Developer: Softrix (Swaitank Jha)
Privacy contact: swaitankj@gmail.com

2. Information stored on your device

Expense Flow stores the financial records you create in the app locally on your device. Depending on the features you use, these records may include:

  • Transactions and transaction amounts;
  • Categories, budgets, and expense summaries; and
  • Other expense-tracking details you enter or save in the app.

These records are not uploaded to our servers. They remain on your device unless you choose to export, share, back up, or otherwise transfer them using a device or app feature.

3. SMS access and on-device transaction detection

If you grant the SMS permission and use the SMS transaction feature, Expense Flow reads supported bank or payment SMS messages to identify transaction information, such as the amount and whether money was credited or debited. This helps the app suggest a transaction for you to review and add.

  • SMS messages are processed locally on your device.
  • SMS message contents are not sent to our servers, to the live-rates provider, to the feedback service, or to any other third party.
  • SMS access is used only for this transaction-detection feature. It is not used for advertising, analytics, or any other purpose.
  • The app uses the detected details only for expense tracking, and you decide whether to add the suggested transaction.

SMS access is optional. You can use Expense Flow without granting it by adding transactions manually, and you can revoke the permission at any time in Android settings.

4. Live rates and external API requests

Expense Flow can request live information such as gold, silver, petrol, or diesel rates from a third-party API provider. To return the requested information, the provider receives the network request. Like most internet services, the provider or network infrastructure may process technical information associated with a request, such as an IP address, request time, or basic connection metadata, under its own privacy practices.

These requests are used only to retrieve rates. They do not include your transaction history, your SMS contents, or any other personal expense data.

5. Feedback submissions

If you choose to submit feedback, the feedback content and any details you include are sent to the online service the app uses to receive feedback. That service may also process technical information needed to receive or protect the submission. Please do not include bank account numbers, passwords, full SMS messages, or other sensitive information in feedback.

Feedback is used to review suggestions, investigate reported problems, and improve Expense Flow. Submitting feedback is entirely optional.

6. Accounts, retention, and deleting your data

Expense Flow does not require you to create an account, so there is no account deletion process.

You can remove locally stored app data through Android’s app settings by clearing the app’s storage, or by uninstalling Expense Flow. These actions may permanently remove your transactions and other saved records, so export or back up anything you want to keep first.

Feedback you have already submitted is not removed when you uninstall the app or clear its data. To request deletion of a submitted feedback item, email swaitankj@gmail.com and describe the submission without including unnecessary sensitive information.

7. Android and device backups

Android may include some app data in device or Google account backups, depending on your device and account settings. Uninstalling the app does not necessarily remove every backup copy. You can manage or delete backups in your device’s backup settings, and you should export important records separately if you want to preserve them.

8. Sharing and sale of information

We do not sell your data. We do not collect or receive your transaction records or SMS message contents. Information is sent to external services only as needed for the online features described above: fetching live rates and submitting feedback. Those providers process requests under their own terms and privacy policies.

9. Security

Expense Flow keeps expense records in the app’s local storage rather than in a central account database. Protect your device with a screen lock, keep Android and the app updated, and avoid sharing exported records with people or services you do not trust. No method of storage or transmission can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

10. Children’s privacy

Expense Flow is a general expense-tracking tool and is not directed at children. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has submitted personal information through the feedback feature, contact us at the email below and we will delete it.

11. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy when app features, permissions, service providers, or applicable requirements change. The latest version will be published on this page with an updated date.
